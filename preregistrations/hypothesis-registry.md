## Corrections

- Entry 12, superseded wording: "Qwen3.6-27B +3.6pp, p=0.0133, survives clustering ([+0.9,+4.9]pp, all LORO folds positive)". "All LORO folds positive" is true of the point estimates only; the paper's leave-django-out result is +2.2pp, 13/7, p=0.26, n=268 (results/reanalysis2/t5_loo.md), and the confirmatory test survives Holm only under the exact test and the wild cluster bootstrap (results/reanalysis2/t3_t4_holm_itt.md). The interval [+0.9,+4.9] is the repository-cluster percentile bootstrap of results/core/paired_contrasts.md, which the paper does not use for inference: with 12 very unequal clusters it reports the wild cluster bootstrap-t, CR2 and the repository sign-flip, and the wild-cluster interval is [+0.5,+5.2] (results/reanalysis/t1_clustered.md).
- Entry 13, superseded wording: "clean Qwen3.6-27B +4.8pp@K=4 → +9.2pp@K=40, within-model difference-in-differences p=0.0003". These numbers and the paper's come from the same Qwen3.6-27B runs (reports `qs2_k4_*`, `qwen36_k12_*`, `qs2_k40_*` in data/swe-verified/grading/summary/); they are different estimands, recomputed from the released data:
  - +4.8pp (K=4) and +9.2pp (K=40) are intention-to-treat estimates over all 500 instances with never-submitted instances scored as unresolved (K=4: 43 vs 67 resolved, 31/7, p=1.2e-4; K=40: 44 vs 90, 54/8, p=1.7e-9; results/reanalysis2/t3_t4_holm_itt.md). The paper's headline estimand excludes never-submitted instances pairwise: +4.9pp (n=466) and +10.1pp (n=457) (results/reanalysis/t2_dose.md). The paper quotes +9.2pp as the intention-to-treat check.
  - p=0.0003 matches the instance-level test of the K=40 versus K=12 contrast (ΔΔ=+6.36pp, n=456, p=2.7e-4), not K=40 versus K=4. The paper's growth test is K=4 to K=40: ΔΔ=+5.2pp on the 425 instances paired at both budgets, instance p=0.0056, wild p=0.012, CR2 p=0.007, sign-flip p=0.063 (results/reanalysis/t2_dose.md). Under intention to treat the K=40 versus K=4 ΔΔ is +4.4pp (p=0.009).
  - The paper does not claim monotone growth: ΔΔ(12−4) = −1.5pp (p=0.36), and the K=40 point depends on 32 ON and 12 OFF never-submitted instances (adversarial bound +2.8pp, p=0.18).
